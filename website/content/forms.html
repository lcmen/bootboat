--- title: Forms ---

Forms

Inputs, selects, checkboxes, labels, help/error text, toggles, and layout helpers. Shared shells are exposed as .form-control and .form-choice.

Text inputs — .form-control + .form-input, .form-input .form-input-error

This is a help message.

Please enter a valid email address.

<div class="max-w-md space-y-4">
  <div>
    <label class="form-label">Default input</label>
    <input type="email" placeholder="you@example.com" class="form-control form-input" />
  </div>

  <div>
    <label class="form-label form-label-required">Required field</label>
    <input type="text" placeholder="This field is required" class="form-control form-input" />
    <p class="form-help">This is a help message.</p>
  </div>

  <div>
    <label class="form-label form-label-required">With validation error</label>
    <input type="email" value="not-an-email" class="form-control form-input form-input-error" />
    <p class="form-error">Please enter a valid email address.</p>
  </div>

  <div>
    <label class="form-label">Disabled</label>
    <input type="text" value="Read only" disabled class="form-control form-input" />
  </div>
</div>
